# Methodological Sources

Read this page only to trace the philosophical basis for fact entry and action boundaries, review a methodological failure, or answer a request for the original source. Daily work follows the short kernel in `SKILL.md` so quotations do not become repeated prompt text.

## Investigation Before Judgment

> “你对于某个问题没有调查，就停止你对于某个问题的发言权。”
>
> — Mao Zedong, *Against Book Worship* (1930)

The original states that speaking without investigating a problem's actual and historical conditions is empty talk. Engineering consequence: do not claim risk, cause, or governance outcome before reading current code, configuration, baseline, history, and command output.

## Find Regularity In Facts

> “‘实事’就是客观存在着的一切事物，‘是’就是客观事物的内部联系，即规律性，‘求’就是我们去研究。”
>
> — Mao Zedong, *Reform Our Study* (1941)

`AVAILABLE`, `PARTIAL`, and `UNAVAILABLE` are fact states. Do not turn unknown into clean through defaults, stale snapshots, or wording.

## Focus On The Principal Contradiction

> “捉住了这个主要矛盾，一切问题就迎刃而解了。”
>
> — Mao Zedong, *On Contradiction* (1937)

Choose one evidenced corruption pattern that can close a loop: backscan, repair, calibrate, and defend it. Do not promote every signal to a gate at once.

## Analyze Concrete Contradictions Concretely

> “For concrete things, concrete analysis” is “the most essential thing in Marxism, the living soul of Marxism.”
>
> — Mao Zedong, *On Contradiction* (1937)

The same name, path, metric, or symptom does not prove the same cause. Distinguish object identity, producer, scope, lifecycle, and observation conditions first. A rule, metric, or experience applies only within its declared conditions; outside them, investigate rather than extrapolate.

## Correct Understanding Through Practice

> “实践、认识、再实践、再认识，这种形式，循环往复以至无穷。”
>
> — Mao Zedong, *On Practice* (1937)

Refactors, rules, and metrics are not completed by confidence. Use worktree or index facts, fixtures, tests, and backscan results, then update the judgment or experience accordingly.

## Test General Guidance Through Particular Practice

> “General calls and particular guidance must be combined.”
>
> — Mao Zedong, *Some Questions Concerning Methods of Leadership* (1943)

A general Skill supplies investigation questions and a minimal loop, not one project's symptoms or repair. Preserve a positive example, legal alternative, and unavailable boundary for each new rule, then backscan local real samples. Promote only conclusions that hold under differing conditions; a single-project repair remains project experience.

## Anti-Corruption Is Protracted Defense

> “兵民是胜利之本。”
>
> — Mao Zedong, *On Protracted War* (1938)

The engineering consequence is not a one-time scan or refactor. Humans, Agents, scripts, tests, and collaboration documents must consolidate each local result into the next starting point. Reject both quick-victory thinking and passive waiting: concentrate on the present principal contradiction, build and backscan the smallest verifiable defense, then extend or calibrate it against new facts.
